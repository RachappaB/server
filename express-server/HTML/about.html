My Story

Why I Built This

I am the founder of Data Sunita platform. I build systems focused on productivity tracking, telemetry analytics, cybersecurity learning, and personal data intelligence.

My goal is to create self-hosted platforms that help people understand time usage, attention, habits, and device behaviour using real data — not synthetic metrics from apps that profit off your attention.

I believe the most powerful thing you can do in the modern world is own your own data. Not surrender it to platforms in exchange for a free service. Not let algorithms decide what you see, think, or do next.
